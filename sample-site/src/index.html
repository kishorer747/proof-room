One oven, one long table

Mara Ellison started Lantern in the old lamp shop on Wick Lane and kept the name, and the brass lantern over the door. The bakers shape everything by hand at one long table, in view of the counter.

When a shelf is empty, that bake is finished for the day. Come earlier tomorrow, or ask us to set a loaf aside.
